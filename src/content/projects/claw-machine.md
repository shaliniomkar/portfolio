A 3-axis claw machine with a manual control system.

## How it works

- Kinematic linkages give precise X, Y and Z movement of the gripper.
- Components were modeled in Siemens NX.
- A gantry, fluid power and mechanical advantage systems were combined to get the prototype performing well.
