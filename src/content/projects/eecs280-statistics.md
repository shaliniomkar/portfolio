A command-line program that reads columns from a data file and summarizes them.

## What it does

- Computes descriptive statistics: count, sum, mean, standard deviation, median, min, max and percentiles.
- Runs a two-sample analysis: a 95% confidence interval for the difference in means between two groups.
- For example, on survey data about how couples meet, it compares the ages of people who met their partner online with those who didn't.

## Concepts

C++ fundamentals, control flow, `std::vector`, procedural abstraction and unit testing.
