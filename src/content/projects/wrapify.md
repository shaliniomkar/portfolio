A full-stack web app for a mid-year look at your Spotify listening habits.

## Features

- Secure sign-in with Spotify OAuth, using the Spotify Web API for your data.
- Your top tracks and top artists.
- A **Listening Age**, based on the release years of the songs you play.
- An **Artist Loyalty Score** that measures how much you stick with your favorite artists.
- One click saves your top tracks as a new playlist in your Spotify account.
- A responsive Bootstrap interface with asynchronous API requests, so pages stay snappy.
